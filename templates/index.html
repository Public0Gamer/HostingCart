Frequently Asked Questions

How fast will my account be activated after payment?

Our server provisioning engine is 100% automated. As soon as your UPI or card payment succeeds, our background worker creates your cPanel/hPanel account, binds your DNS zones, activates free SSL, and issues credentials in under 10 seconds.

Can I use my existing domain from GoDaddy or Namecheap?

Yes, absolutely! Simply change your domain's nameservers at your registrar to: ns1.hostingcart.in and ns2.hostingcart.in. Your domain will automatically route to your HostingCart account.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), RuPay, Visa, MasterCard debit & credit cards, Net Banking, and instant QR Code payments.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes! We provide an unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee on all web hosting plans. Per standard ICANN registry regulations, domain name registration fees (or wholesale domain value if included as a Free Domain) are non-refundable. In case of cancellation, you will receive a full refund for the hosting service and retain 100% ownership of your registered domain name for the entire 1-year registration period.
